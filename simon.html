An Arranged Beginning

What began with a simple exchange of biodatas soon became something neither of us expected. Our first call was a little nervous, but the next night, a two-and-a-half-hour conversation changed everything. We talked about marriage, dreams and expectations, and found a comfort that felt surprisingly natural.

Became a Love we chose

Two weeks later, Sudhanva came to Kochi, and we finally met. From café to waterfalls, and quiet moments together, those two days made us feel comfortable, safe and connected. As the days passed, our conversations grew deeper and our bond stronger. Somewhere along the way, an arranged beginning became a love we chose, and a person we knew we wanted beside us for all the chapters ahead.
